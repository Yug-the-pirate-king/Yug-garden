02 // Flagship Case Study

HimDrishti — Polar Navigation Decision Support

An AI-driven decision support system for Antarctic sea-ice forecasting and iceberg trajectory prediction. Built for Smart India Hackathon.

🔥 PyTorch & Attention-LSTM ⚡ FastAPI & A* Pathfinding
Smart India Hackathon 2026

Navigating Extreme Waters. Predicting Sea-Ice Dynamics.

Traditional maritime routing in polar regions relies on lagging meteorological reports and static ice-edge approximations, increasing vessel collision risks. With HimDrishti, we engineered a full-stack decision support platform combining XGBoost anomaly flags with Attention-LSTM time-series forecasting and real-time A* pathfinding over FastAPI to deliver dynamic, collision-free transit corridors.
